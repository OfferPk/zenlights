## Full Description (max 4000 characters)

Find focus, balance, and quiet satisfaction with ZenLights — an elegant, calming offline Lights Out grid illumination logic puzzle designed to soothe your mind while sharpening your spatial reasoning.

Your objective is simple yet deeply meditative: extinguish all lit nodes on the grid to restore complete tranquility. Tapping any node toggles its state between illuminated and dark, while harmonically toggling its four orthogonal neighbors (Up, Down, Left, and Right).

With 50 solver-verified levels spanning five progressive difficulty packs — plus an infinite Zen Free Play generator — ZenLights offers hours of mindful problem-solving.

Whether enjoying a quiet evening, taking a mindful pause during a hectic day, or playing offline while traveling, ZenLights provides a pure, distraction-free sanctuary.

### 🌟 Game Highlights:

- **100% Offline Gameplay**: Zero internet or Wi-Fi needed. Play without interruptions anytime, anywhere — on planes, subways, or remote escapes.
- **50 Handcrafted & Solver-Verified Levels**: Journey through 5 distinct level packs:
  • Pack 1: Dawn Awakening (3x3 grids) — Gentle ripples to introduce orthogonal wave dynamics
  • Pack 2: Lotus Petals (4x4 grids) — Balanced symmetries and floral geometric motifs
  • Pack 3: Constellations (5x5 grids) — Classic deep logic matrices and starry patterns
  • Pack 4: Eclipse Matrix (5x5 & 6x6 grids) — Complex harmonic interference puzzles
  • Pack 5: Infinite Zen (6x6 grids) — Grand meditative master challenges
- **Zen Free Play Mode**: Generate endless custom solvable puzzles on 3x3, 4x4, 5x5, or 6x6 boards with selectable difficulty tiers (Light, Balanced, Master).
- **Exact Linear Solver & Hint Assistant**: Powered by a GF(2) Gaussian elimination engine that can instantly calculate the minimal move path and highlight the optimal next step whenever you need guidance.
- **Procedural Web Audio Synthesis**: Experience crystal bell chimes and Tibetan singing bowl overtones tuned to a meditative pentatonic scale that shifts dynamically based on grid position.
- **4 Bioluminescent Themes**: Choose between Deep Nebula, Emerald Bamboo, Solar Amber, and Amethyst Twilight to suit your visual mood.
- **Optimal Par Move Scoring**: Aim for the 3-star rating on every puzzle by matching or beating mathematically minimal move counts.
- **Unlimited Undo & Instant Reset**: Experiment fearlessly with unrestricted undo and quick reset buttons.
- **Zero Ads, Zero Distractions**: No pop-ups, no banners, no paywalls, and no in-app purchases. Pure logic and serenity.
